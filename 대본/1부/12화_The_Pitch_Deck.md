## The Pitch Deck (피치덱)

> **표기 전용 정보 (낭독하지 않음)**
> - 부/화: 1부 12화 · 서사 기능: 첫 피치덱 제작, Noah와 Lily의 갈등과 화해
> - 배경: 11화 사흘 뒤 목요일 밤 10시 ~ 새벽 3시, Capital BioWorks 회의실(24시간 출입)
> - 등장인물: Noah, Lily, Priya, Elena(전화)

## 대본

### 도입 — 회의실, 밤 10시

LILY: Okay. I brought my laptop, my tablet, and three energy drinks.

NOAH: And I brought tacos. As promised. Your design fee.

LILY: Tacos are a deposit. Not a fee.

PRIYA: She's already negotiating. I like her more every day.

LILY: Thanks, Priya. So, what are we making?

NOAH: A pitch deck. Ten slides. For investors.

LILY: When do you need it?

NOAH: Elena's trying to get us a meeting next week.

LILY: Next week. So, tonight.

PRIYA: Welcome to startups.

LILY: First question, before I design anything. Why is the company called Veyra? Nobody told me, and I can't draw a good logo for a name if I don't know the story behind it.

PRIYA: Elena suggested "Vera." It means "true" in Latin.

NOAH: But vera dot com was taken. So we added a Y.

LILY: So the company name is "truth plus a domain problem."

NOAH: When you say it like that, it sounds less inspiring.

### 장면1 — 덱의 구조

PRIYA: Okay, structure first. What goes on ten slides?

NOAH: Slide one, the title. Our name, our logo, one sentence.

LILY: Logo. Can I use the napkin drawing? From the hospital?

NOAH: You still have it?

LILY: I scanned it. I've been cleaning it up for two weeks.

PRIYA: Show us.

LILY: Here. A net. But one knot is open. Like it's letting go.

NOAH: Lily. That's beautiful.

PRIYA: It's exactly the chemistry. A link designed to release.

LILY: I know. I read your chapter four. Well, the pictures.

NOAH: Okay. Logo, done. Slide two, the problem.

PRIYA: Slide three, the solution. Slide four, how it works.

NOAH: Five, the market. Six, the competition. Seven, the business model.

LILY: What's a business model?

NOAH: How we make money. Who pays, how much, and how often. For us, clinics would buy the filler, and if they like it, they keep ordering it again and again.

PRIYA: Eight, what we've done so far. Our traction.

LILY: Traction. Like tires on a road?

NOAH: Kind of. Proof that we're moving. Not spinning in place.

PRIYA: To be honest, our traction is small. Twelve interviews and one autoclave test, which is still running. That's not much, so we need to present it clearly and not pretend it's more.

NOAH: It's early. Investors know that.

LILY: Nine?

NOAH: The team. The four of us.

LILY: And ten?

NOAH: The ask. One point five million dollars, and what it gets us. Investors don't just want a number, they want to know exactly what their money will help us prove.

LILY: Okay. That's a clear structure. Even I understand it.

PRIYA: That's the test. If Lily understands it, an investor will.

LILY: I'm choosing to take that as a compliment.

NOAH: Now the one sentence for slide one. The tagline.

PRIYA: "Enzyme-sensitive crosslinked hyaluronic acid for rapid reversal."

LILY: Nobody will remember that. Ever.

NOAH: "Long-lasting filler with a built-in emergency exit."

LILY: Better. Still too long.

PRIYA: Then what would you say, Lily?

LILY: "The filler you can take back."

NOAH: That's it. That's the line.

### 장면2 — 슬라이드 피드백

PRIYA: It's midnight. Let's look at the problem slide.

NOAH: Here's my draft. Six bullet points. Statistics. A chart. And a quote.

PRIYA: Honestly? This slide is doing too much.

NOAH: Too much? It's all important.

PRIYA: If everything is important, nothing is. An investor will look at this slide for a few seconds, so it has to say one clear thing. Right now it says six things at once.

LILY: She's right. My eyes don't know where to go.

NOAH: Okay. What should stay?

PRIYA: One number. Nine out of twelve.

LILY: And one quote. A human voice.

NOAH: Jasmine's quote. "For ten seconds, I froze."

PRIYA: Without her name, though. We promised every person we interviewed that we'd keep them anonymous, and if we break that promise on slide two, nobody in this industry will talk to us again.

NOAH: Of course. "A nurse injector, Austin."

LILY: One number, one quote, lots of white space. Much better.

NOAH: Next. The solution slide.

PRIYA: Here I'd like a simple diagram. The net. A knot opening.

LILY: Like the logo, but animated? First tight, then releasing?

PRIYA: Yes. Can you do that?

LILY: By two a.m. If nobody talks to me for an hour.

NOAH: Deal. Priya, while she works, the market slide.

PRIYA: How big is the filler market?

NOAH: In the US, several billion dollars a year. And growing.

PRIYA: Define "several." Investors will ask.

NOAH: I'll get the exact number from public reports, not from Halvern files. Using anything from my old job would be a huge mistake, and it would hurt us more than a vague number.

PRIYA: Good. Clean hands.

NOAH: Always. Diane would sense it from across town.

PRIYA: And competition? Halvern, obviously.

NOAH: Halvern and three other big players. All long-lasting. None reversible by design.

PRIYA: "Yet." Remember, the idea is public. They could copy it.

NOAH: Then our edge is speed, focus, and better formulas. The big players have many products to worry about. We have only one, so every hour we work goes into making it reversible and safe.

PRIYA: And a team that actually cares. Put that on slide nine.

### 절정·전환 — 새벽 2시, Lily의 반대

NOAH: Priya went home. It's two a.m. How's the animation?

LILY: Done. Look. Tight net. Then the enzyme. Then the knot opens.

NOAH: That's perfect. Okay, one more idea for the problem slide.

LILY: You already fixed the problem slide.

NOAH: What if we add a photo? Of you, in the ER. That night.

LILY: A photo of me?

NOAH: Investors remember stories, Lil. They see hundreds of decks with charts and numbers, and they forget most of them. But a real face, a real night in the ER, makes the problem real.

LILY: My gray nose. On a slide. For strangers.

NOAH: It's powerful, Lil.

LILY: I'm not your marketing story, Noah.

NOAH: That's not what I meant.

LILY: It's exactly what you meant. You did the marketing voice.

NOAH: Okay. Maybe I did. I'm sorry.

LILY: I still look in the mirror every morning, Noah. I still see the mark, even when nobody else can. So putting that night on a slide isn't a story to me. It's my face.

NOAH: I didn't know it still bothered you that much.

LILY: Because you never asked. You asked about the logo, the animation, the tagline. Not about me. For two weeks I've been helping you, and you haven't once asked how I'm doing.

NOAH: You're right. How are you, Lil? Really?

LILY: Some days fine. Some days I wear a lot of concealer.

NOAH: The photo is out. Completely. I promise.

LILY: Thank you.

NOAH: Can I say "my sister" when I pitch? Just the words. No details.

LILY: The words, yes. My face, no. My nose, definitely no.

NOAH: Deal. Words only.

LILY: And Noah? I'm proud of you. Even when you do the marketing voice.

NOAH: That might be the nicest thing you've ever said to me.

### ② 비즈니스 컬처 노트 — 10장짜리 피치덱
실리콘밸리에서 널리 쓰이는 기본 구조는 **10장 내외**다. ① 표지(회사명·한 줄 소개) ② 문제 ③ 해결책 ④ 작동 원리/제품 ⑤ 시장 규모 ⑥ 경쟁 ⑦ 비즈니스 모델 ⑧ 성과(traction) ⑨ 팀 ⑩ 투자 요청(the ask)이다. 투자자는 덱 하나를 평균 몇 분 안에 훑어보기 때문에 "슬라이드 하나에 메시지 하나"가 원칙이다. 또 미국에서는 고객 인터뷰 내용을 인용할 때 사전 동의와 익명 처리를 매우 중요하게 여긴다.

### ③ 실무 템플릿 — 피치덱 10장 구조

| # | 슬라이드 | 핵심 질문 | Veyra 예시 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Title | Who are you, in one line? | Veyra — *The filler you can take back.* |
| 2 | Problem | What hurts? | *9 out of 12 injectors faced a vascular occlusion.* |
| 3 | Solution | What's your answer? | A long-lasting filler that dissolves fast in an emergency. |
| 4 | How it works | Why does it work? | A net with knots designed to release. |
| 5 | Market | How big? | US dermal filler market (public data). |
| 6 | Competition | Who else? Why you? | Big players: long-lasting, not reversible by design. |
| 7 | Business model | Who pays, how much, how often? | Clinics buy syringes; repeat orders. |
| 8 | Traction | What have you proven? | 12 interviews, autoclave test in progress. |
| 9 | Team | Why you? | Science, manufacturing, clinical, market. |
| 10 | The ask | How much, for what? | $1.5M for 18 months to prove the technology. |

### ④ 긴 발화 분석

> **PRIYA (장면2):** "If everything is important, nothing is. An investor will look at this slide for a few seconds, so it has to say one clear thing. Right now it says six things at once."

| 구조 | 영어 | 기능 |
|---|---|---|
| ① 원칙(격언) | If everything is important, nothing is. | 피드백의 핵심을 한 줄 원칙으로 먼저 제시 |
| ② 근거(청중의 현실) | An investor will look at this slide for a few seconds, | 왜 그 원칙이 중요한지 듣는 사람 입장에서 설명 |
| ③ 기준 제시 | so it has to say one clear thing. | 좋은 슬라이드의 기준을 구체적으로 제시 |
| ④ 현재 진단 | Right now it says six things at once. | 기준과 현재 상태의 차이를 숫자로 보여 줌 |

**활용 패턴:** 원칙 → 청중의 현실 → 기준 → "Right now…" 진단
**연결 표현:** so(그래서) · has to(~해야 한다) · Right now(지금은) · at once(한꺼번에)
**연습:** 동료의 보고서 초안에 같은 구조로 30단어 안팎의 피드백을 해 보기.
