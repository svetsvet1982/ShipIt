## 대본

ZOE: Then we go in with a doctor. And a plan. What's the plan?

### 장면1 — 오후 2시, Lumi Clinic 본원

BAE: Ms. Kim. Ms. Whitman. Dr. Choi. Sit. I'll be brief. Your price is too high.

GRACE: Thank you for your time, Dr. Bae. May I ask what you'd consider reasonable?

BAE: One hundred forty thousand. For twenty branches. Or I buy Bellacare at eighty-five.

ZOE: That's a forty percent discount.

BAE: That's a Korean price. Your American price works in America. Here, there are fifty fillers.

GRACE: I understand, Dr. Bae. But if we cut to one-forty, Bellacare goes to seventy, and then we're under pressure to go to one-twenty. Every cut invites another cut, and nobody stops until someone runs out of money.

ZOE: That's a race to the bottom. We won't join it. Not because we're proud. Because nobody wins it.

BAE: Somebody wins. The clinic wins. Cheaper syringes. Bigger margin.

CHOI: Seung-ho. May I? As a doctor. Not as their speaker.

BAE: You're on their stage now, Min-jun. But go ahead.

CHOI: How many vascular events did Lumi have last year? All twenty branches?

BAE: …That's internal data.

CHOI: I'm not asking for the report. Just the number. Between doctors.

BAE: Eleven. All recovered. Mostly.

CHOI: And where? Which areas?

BAE: Nine were nose or forehead. Two were lips.

CHOI: Nose, forehead, glabella. Those areas are thirty percent of filler volume in Korea, but eighty percent of serious complications, because the vessels there are small and connected to the eye. Your nine cases fit the national pattern exactly, Seung-ho. That isn't bad luck. That's anatomy.

GRACE: That's from the Korean Dermatological Society data. Appendix C of our launch plan.

BAE: So you want me to switch everything to Veyra? At three times the price?

CHOI: No. Use Bellacare for cheeks and lips, where the risk is low and price matters more. Use Veyra where the risk is highest. You don't need to pay premium prices everywhere, Seung-ho, only in the places where one mistake becomes a disaster.

ZOE: …We didn't plan that.

GRACE: Zoe. Quiet. Let it land.

BAE: Nose and forehead only. That's thirty percent of my volume. Not a hundred.

CHOI: And eighty percent of your midnight phone calls. And your lawsuits.

BAE: Do you know what one blindness case costs a clinic in Gangnam? Not the lawsuit. The reputation. One post online, one news story, and patients stop booking at every branch, not just the one where it happened. Twenty branches means twenty times the exposure.

CHOI: I know. That's why I'm here.

BAE: Ms. Kim. Even for thirty percent, two hundred thirty is too high.

ZOE: Then let's talk about what the price includes. Not just the syringe.

GRACE: Korean Veyra Ready training for every injector, free of charge. An emergency kit in every treatment room. A Korean-language hotline, twenty-four hours a day, so your doctors never face an occlusion alone at three in the morning. Bellacare sells you a syringe. We're offering you a safety system.

ZOE: And serial scanning. Every syringe traced. If a regulator asks, you have the answer in one minute.

BAE: Bellacare offers a free lunch and a calendar.

GRACE: Then the comparison is a calendar versus a hotline at three a.m.

BAE: …You negotiate like your mother, Ms. Kim. Mr. Park told me about her.

GRACE: Korea is small.

BAE: Send me a proposal. Nose, forehead, glabella. Twenty branches. With a number I can show my partners.

### 장면2 — 오후 5시, 강남 사무실

YUNA: Hello! Sorry I'm late. Traffic in Gangnam is a crime. Literally, there should be a law.

ZOE: Thank you for coming. Three point two million subscribers. You're the biggest name on our list.

YUNA: You're the only filler brand that didn't send me a free treatment. That's why I came.

GRACE: We can't. Korean medical law. No treatment testimonials in advertising. Not even from influencers.

ZOE: In America, that's the whole influencer model. "I tried it, I love it."

YUNA: In Korea, that gets you a fine. And a lot of angry comments. Both.

GRACE: And medical ads need pre-review. By a committee. Before anything is published.

ZOE: So what can we do? Without a testimonial? Without the brand?

YUNA: Education. My viewers trust me because I don't sell. I teach. Mostly skincare, sometimes my own mistakes. The moment I start selling a product, I lose the one thing that makes my channel valuable, so whatever we do has to help them even if they never buy anything.

HAEUN: The "Two Questions" video? Our idea?

YUNA: I read it. "Before any filler, ask two questions. Can it be dissolved? How fast?"

ZOE: No brand name. Just the questions. Like London.

YUNA: And I'll tell my own story. Not a testimonial. A warning. Three years ago. My forehead.

GRACE: You had a complication?

YUNA: The skin went grey, right here, and I was terrified. My doctor recognised it and dissolved it fast, so I was lucky. But I never asked about dissolving before the treatment. Most of my viewers won't ask that question either.

ZOE: You've never told your viewers?

YUNA: I was ashamed. Beauty people aren't supposed to have accidents. That's the problem, isn't it?

GRACE: That's exactly the problem. Safety is assumed. Until it isn't.

YUNA: Pay me for the education video. Not for selling. And send it to the review committee first.

ZOE: Deal. Grace, legal review tomorrow. Yuna, you just became my favourite person in Korea.

### ② 비즈니스 컬처 노트 — 가격 전쟁에서 프리미엄 지키기
저가 경쟁사가 가격을 내릴 때 따라 내리면 **바닥으로의 경주**가 시작되고, 마진뿐 아니라 **다른 시장의 가격 기준**까지 무너진다. 대안은 세 가지다. ① **세그먼트를 좁힌다**: 모든 시술이 아니라 위험이 가장 큰 부위처럼 **가격보다 결과가 중요한 영역**을 공략한다. ② **가격 대신 포함된 가치**를 말한다: 교육, 응급 키트, 24시간 핫라인, 추적 시스템. ③ **양보는 서면화된 공통 규칙으로**: 개별 할인 대신 모두에게 같은 볼륨 등급을 적용하고, 안전 조건(인증 기한)을 붙인다. 한국에서는 **의료 광고 사전 심의**와 **시술 경험담 광고 금지** 때문에 미국식 인플루언서 후기 모델을 쓸 수 없으며, 브랜드 없는 **안전 교육 콘텐츠**가 신뢰를 쌓는 현실적 경로다.

### ④ 긴 발화 분석

> **GRACE (절정·전환):** "Here's one. Bellacare dropped to eighty-five last week, and today Ha-eun heard seventy-nine. At that price, they're almost certainly losing money on every syringe. A price war only works if you can outlast your opponent, and we don't need to, because we're not in their race."

| 구조 | 영어 | 기능 |
|---|---|---|
| ① 요청에 응답 | Here's one. | "비즈니스 논리도 필요하다"는 요구에 바로 답함 |
| ② 사실(숫자 추이) | Bellacare dropped to eighty-five last week, and today Ha-eun heard seventy-nine. | 경쟁사 가격 변화를 시점과 함께 제시 |
| ③ 추론(헤지) | At that price, they're almost certainly losing money on every syringe. | almost certainly로 확신의 정도를 조절하며 해석 |
| ④ 전략 결론(원리 + 이유) | A price war only works if you can outlast your opponent, and we don't need to, because we're not in their race. | 일반 원리 → 우리에게 적용 → because로 마무리 |

**활용 패턴:** Here's one → 숫자 추이 → 헤지한 해석 → only works if… + 우리의 입장
**연결 표현:** almost certainly(거의 확실히) · only works if(~할 때만 통한다) · outlast(~보다 오래 버티다)
**연습:** 경쟁사가 할인 공세를 벌일 때 "우리도 내리자"는 동료에게, 숫자를 근거로 같은 구조로 40~50단어로 반대해 보기.
