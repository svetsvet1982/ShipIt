### 장면1 — 10월 29일 오후, 상하이 Meiheng Medical

ZHOU: Welcome to Shanghai, Ms. Kim. And Ms. Lee. We've prepared tea, and a very long presentation.

GRACE: Thank you. May I ask one thing before the presentation? What do you need from us?

ZHOU: …That's usually the last question, not the first.

GRACE: I learned it from my CEO. He learned it from a napkin.

ZHOU: Then let me answer honestly. Our clinics are losing patients to cheap fillers sold online, many of them fake. We need a product our doctors can prove is genuine, in front of the patient, in five seconds. Your scan system does that. Without the system, your filler is just another expensive syringe.

SUJIN: So the system matters more to you than the filler?

ZHOU: The system is why the filler sells. That's why we want to own it here.

GRACE: I understand. And here's why we can't give you that. If the database is on your servers, then every competitor will say Veyra's proof is controlled by its distributor. The day your interests and the patient's interests are different, nobody will trust the scan. That includes your own doctors.

ZHOU: Chinese law says the data must stay in China. You can't keep it in Korea.

SUJIN: We know. We'll host it in China, with a licensed local cloud provider. Owned by Veyra Asia's China entity.

ZHOU: And what do we see?

GRACE: Each clinic sees its own scans. You see a dashboard of every clinic. Names stay with the treating clinic.

ZHOU: That's less than I asked for.

GRACE: It's more than any distributor has. Including our Korean ones.

ZHOU: Ten-year exclusive?

GRACE: I'll answer that on Friday, after my board. But one more thing first. Your clinic list.

SUJIN: Dr. Ruiz reviewed it last night. Thirteen hundred of your nine thousand are "life beauty institutions." Not medical clinics.

ZHOU: They're legal businesses. Very profitable. Many have a doctor who visits.

GRACE: A visiting doctor isn't a medical clinic. In our joint venture, sales to non-medical businesses need the approval of our Chief Medical Officer, and she has already said no. It's not my preference. It's in our articles, and I can't change it.

ZHOU: So seven thousand seven hundred.

GRACE: Seven thousand seven hundred. Still the largest launch Veyra has ever had.

LIN: May I join? Lin Hui, dermatology, at a teaching hospital here. Mr. Zhou asked me to lead your trial.

GRACE: Welcome, Professor Lin. Thank you.

LIN: I heard the last part. About the beauty institutions. I agree with Dr. Ruiz.

ZHOU: Professor Lin, you're supposed to be on my side.

LIN: I'm on the patients' side, Mr. Zhou. Last year, my department treated forty-three patients with complications from fillers injected outside hospitals. Eleven involved products we couldn't identify. If Veyra enters through those institutions, the first counterfeit case will have Veyra's name on it.

ZHOU: …Seven thousand seven hundred. Fine.

SUJIN: And the trial, Professor? What do Chinese regulators need from us?

LIN: A local trial. About two hundred patients, twelve months' follow-up. Korean and Japanese data help, but can't replace Chinese patients.

GRACE: Like Japan. Data has no accent, but regulators want local faces.

LIN: Exactly. If your ethics committee documents are ready, we can apply next week.

SUJIN: They're ready. In Chinese and English. I translated the last section on the plane.

ZHOU: Ms. Kim, you came with fewer people than I expected. And you said no more than I expected.

GRACE: I'd rather say no in October than apologize in March.

ZHOU: Friday, then. I'll wait for your board.

### ② 비즈니스 컬처 노트 — 합작법인에서 의견이 다를 때
합작법인(JV)은 주주가 둘 이상이라 **누가 결정하느냐**가 늘 흐릿하다. 그래서 한쪽 주주가 JV CEO를 건너뛰고 **본사 창업자에게 직접 전화하는** 일이 생긴다. 이때 본사가 응하면 JV CEO의 권한은 그날로 사라진다. 좋은 대응은 ① 전화를 받은 쪽이 **"CEO를 거치세요"**라고 공개적으로 말하고, ② 이견을 막지 말고 **올리는 길**을 만드는 것이다. **에스컬레이션 경로**는 문제의 종류(안전·품질·사업)별로 결정권자를 정하고, 이견이 생기면 **의장 → 이사회** 순으로 올린다. 경로가 정해지면 갈등은 개인 문제가 아니라 **절차**가 된다. 한국·중국 비즈니스에서는 공식 회의 밖의 관계(식사, 사과)가 회복을 마무리하는 경우가 많다.

### ③ 실무 템플릿 — JV 에스컬레이션 경로

> **Veyra Asia — How We Disagree**
> 1. **Safety** (recall · non-medical distribution · training standards) → CMO (veto in articles)
> 2. **Quality** → Head of Quality, in writing
> 3. **Business terms** → CEO decides. Director disagrees? → bring it to the **Chair**
> 4. **Chair can't resolve** → JV Board
> **Rule:** Nobody goes around the path — including the CEO and shareholders' head offices.

### ④ 긴 발화 분석

> **GRACE (장면1):** "I understand. And here's why we can't give you that. If the database is on your servers, then every competitor will say Veyra's proof is controlled by its distributor. The day your interests and the patient's interests are different, nobody will trust the scan. That includes your own doctors."

| 구조 | 영어 | 기능 |
|---|---|---|
| ① 공감 | I understand. | 요구를 들었음을 먼저 인정 |
| ② 거절 예고 | And here's why we can't give you that. | 결론을 먼저, 이유는 뒤에 |
| ③ 조건-결과 1 | If the database is on your servers, then every competitor will say… | 상대 요구가 만들 외부 결과 |
| ④ 조건-결과 2 | The day your interests and the patient's interests are different, nobody will trust the scan. | 시간 표현으로 위험을 구체화 |
| ⑤ 상대 이익과 연결 | That includes your own doctors. | 거절이 상대에게도 이익임을 보여 줌 |

**활용 패턴:** "I understand. Here's why we can't… If…, then… The day…, nobody will… That includes you."
**연결 표현:** here's why(이유는 이렇다) · The day…(~하는 날) · That includes…(거기엔 ~도 포함된다)
**연습:** 고객사가 원본 설계 파일을 요구할 때, 거절하면서 그것이 고객사에도 이익인 이유를 45단어 안팎으로 말하기.
